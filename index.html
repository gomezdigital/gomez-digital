FAQ

Questions before we build?

What kind of projects do you take on? +

We work on business websites, landing pages, website redesigns, practical digital tools, automation workflows, digital branding and selected custom projects.

Do you work with clients outside Botswana? +

Yes. Gomez Digital is based in Botswana and works with clients worldwide. Projects are planned and delivered remotely through clear online communication and agreed milestones.

Can you build a website from scratch? +

Yes. We can take a project from an initial idea and structure through design, development, testing and launch.

Can you improve an existing website? +

Yes. We can redesign an existing website, improve its structure and user experience, make it more responsive, and introduce new functionality.

How do I start a project? +

Send us a short description of what you want to build, your goals and any relevant links or references. We'll use that information to understand the project and determine the next step.
