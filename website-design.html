GOOD TO KNOW

Before we get started.

Do you work with clients outside Botswana?+

Yes. Projects can be planned and delivered remotely with clear communication and agreed milestones.

How much does a website cost?+

Pricing depends on the number of pages, content, features and complexity. After learning about your goals, we'll provide a clear scope and quote before work begins.

Will my website appear at the top of Google?+

No agency can honestly guarantee a particular ranking. We can implement foundational SEO, clear page structure and search-friendly content, while visibility develops over time and depends on competition and other factors.

Can you help after launch?+

Ongoing updates, maintenance and future improvements can be discussed and scoped separately so you understand what is included.
